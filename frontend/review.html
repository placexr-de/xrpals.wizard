REFERENCE

Placement guidance

Recommendations for room geometry and anchor placement.

Room dimensions

For a rectangular room, use length L, width W, and height H.

LengthL = 12 m
WidthW = 9 m
HeightH = 4 m
max(L, W)H ≤ 3

Example: max(12, 9) / 4 = 3. This meets the stated guideline.

Anchor placement

Distribute anchors around the room to give the positioning system a useful spread of reference points.

  • Place anchors around the room perimeter, including corners or walls where appropriate.
  • Keep anchors at least 2 metres apart where possible.
  • Use different heights to reduce measurement ambiguity.
i Anchor geometry affects positioning quality. Avoid clustering anchors in one area or placing all of them at the same height.
